## 📋 Design Guidelines

### Do's ✓
- Use on light or dark backgrounds
- Scale up or down without distortion
- Apply in any single color
- Use negative space creatively

### Don'ts ✗
- Don't distort or skew
- Don't use too many colors at once
- Don't place text too close
- Don't use below 1 inch wide for print
